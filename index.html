Read your LoRAB tag over FM radio

The tag hides its messages in the FM stations around it. Plug in an Airspy and press Start: the page finds the FM stations, finds your tag and starts listening on its own. Everything runs in this page; there is nothing to install.

The first time, Chrome asks which device to use: pick AIRSPY. Next time it starts by itself when the Airspy is plugged in.

Requirements and troubleshooting
  • Browser: Chrome, Edge or Opera (desktop or Android). Firefox and Safari can't talk to USB devices.
  • Address: the page must be opened over https:// or http://localhost.
  • Linux: give your user access to the Airspy once, then replug it: 
    echo 'SUBSYSTEM=="usb", ATTRS{idVendor}=="1d50", ATTRS{idProduct}=="60a1", MODE="0666"' | sudo tee /etc/udev/rules.d/52-airspy.rules
sudo udevadm control --reload-rules
  • Windows: the Airspy must use the WinUSB driver (the official Airspy driver, or install WinUSB with Zadig).
  • macOS, ChromeOS, Android: nothing to install.
  • Close other radio programs (GNU Radio, SDR#, SDR++) first: only one program can use the Airspy at a time.
